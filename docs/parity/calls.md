## The call controls' look (pass 4)

Accept is the accent with the accent glyph; Decline and Hang up are the danger colour (ZapFast's `Danger`, the theme's `Red`) with a white glyph; Mute, the camera and Devices are the active surface with the text colour, and the text colour with the window's while muted, the camera off or the pickers open, as `control()` has them. All are solid in a window that is not the key one (a ringing call's window shown alone never is): `ButtonRecipe`s of the package (`recipes.go`) through `IconButtonOpts.Recipe`, hover and press laying the glyph colour over the fill at 12 % and 24 % (upstream's `blend(fill, tint, 0.12)`), disabled the fill at 60 %. Test: `TestTheCallsControlsAreSolidInAnInactiveWindow` reads the drawn fills in a key and a non-key window; captures `ringing-light|dark.png`, `ringing-group-light|dark.png`, `active-light|dark.png` (ZapFast's themes, window not key).

## The window at its smallest

Upstream's smallest window is 400 × 300 (`src/main.rs:617`) and the call surface is drawn over it, so the call window's minimum is now 400 × 300 (it was 360). Upstream's ringing and live columns (a picture of 24–26 % of the height, at least 96 pt, with fixed gaps) already overrun a 300 pt window, so the Go window compacts where upstream does not: the gap above the picture goes first, then the picture down to 48 pt, only as far as the lines under it need (`avatarFit`). Test: `TestTheCallWindowFitsAtItsSmallestSizeInEveryLanguage` runs the layout audit on ringing (voice, video, group), active, muted, video, camera off and the group's tiles in en/de/ru at 360 × 300, 400 × 300 and 720 × 520; it failed with the subtitle under the answers (21.5 px) and the timer out of its box (19.6 px) before. One allowance: `rexui.Avatar` puts a lone initial (the group's "E") up to 0.8 px off the middle of a picture under 96 pt, a framework gap. Captures `min-ringing-de.png`, `min-active-de.png`.

## Video, as built

Frames come from `core.Frames.Frame(stream)` and go back in the same frame (`video.go`). Per stream: `NewYUVTexture(YUVI420, w, h, BT601Video)` on the first frame and when the size changes (the old texture freed), `StageYUV` with the planes sliced from the frame's one buffer and its strides, skipped while `Frame.PTS` is the one in the texture, `FreeTexture` when the stream's camera goes off, the window goes and the call ends; `k.ExternalImage(stream, tex, w, h, fit, radius, "")` draws it and `k.ImageShown(stream)` gates the frame pull and the 33 ms wake. The textures are on `e.A.R`, the renderer the windows share.
