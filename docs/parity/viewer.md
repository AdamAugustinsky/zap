## Rows owned elsewhere that the viewer serves

- **MK-011** (ui/conversation): a sticker opened from its bubble ("Open in the viewer") shows in the sticker view of this window, as upstream's `show_sticker`: a square card (at most 300 pt, shrunk with the window), the sticker animated (`img.Animate`), **Add to favorites** (`SaveSticker`, toast "Added to favorites") or **Remove from favorites** (`ForgetSticker` on the saved copy), and a click on the card, outside it, or Escape closes. The favorite copy is found as upstream does: the saved file named by the sticker's content hash (read on a worker; the core's `Stickers` list, asked with `RecentStickers`). Tests: TestAStickerShowsOnACardWithAddToFavorites, TestAFavoriteStickerOffersRemoveFromFavorites, TestAStickerClosesOnAClickOnItAndEscape, TestAStickerClosesOnAClickOutsideIt (`ClickAt` on the backdrop), TestStickerViewAllocatesNothing.

## Gaps

- Closing the viewer stops and closes its clip and the bubble shows its poster again, which is what upstream does (`CloseImagePreview`: `video.stop()`, no resume in the bubble), so there is nothing to hand back. What the bubble (`av`) does on its side of the handoff to the viewer: see the report of pass 5 (call `OpenViewerPlayingAt(chat, msg, position)` and stop its own clip).
- New English strings with no upstream msgid, which the translation table does not have: "Photos and videos", "Loading", "Downloading", "Enter Full Screen", "Exit Full Screen", "Seek" (the seek bar's accessible name).
