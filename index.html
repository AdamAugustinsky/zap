WhatsApp, native on your Mac.

Zap is a WhatsApp app built for macOS. It opens in about a tenth of a second, sits in 85 MB while it waits, and keeps your chats on your Mac.

Download for Mac Free · macOS 14 or later · Apple silicon

0%CPU when nothing is happening. No timers, no redraws

Everything you use on your phone

Messages

Formatted text, replies, edits, reactions, forwards, pictures, files, stickers, GIFs, polls and voice messages.

Media in place

Voice messages, videos, round videos, GIFs and animated stickers play right in the chat.

Calls

Voice and video calls, in a window of their own.

Your history

Chats stay in an encrypted archive on your Mac. Older messages come from your phone as you scroll.

Several numbers

Link more than one WhatsApp number and switch between them in one window.

A real Mac app

The menu bar, keyboard shortcuts, notifications, light and dark mode, and text you can select across messages.

Your chats stay with you

Zap links to your phone as a companion device, the way WhatsApp's own desktop app does, and talks to WhatsApp directly. There is no server of ours in between, and no account to make.

Lock chats behind a code, or the whole window behind an app lock.

  1. Open Zap. It shows a QR code.
  2. On your phone, open WhatsApp, then Settings, Linked devices, Link a device, and scan it.
  3. Your recent chats arrive. Older ones follow as you scroll up.
