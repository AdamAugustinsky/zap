## Synchronous-send regression evidence (2026-10-22)

- `CommandSendFiles.encode` permits 64 paths, whereas staging has no total limit. Previously 65 paths failed before entering the core, but the composer cleared the attachments, caption, draft and quote and toasted "Sending 65 files…". No asynchronous refusal could restore them. Text/edit ignored synchronous errors too; image/voice paths discarded their blobs.
- Upstream `src/app.rs::send_pending` transfers caption, mentions and quote once; `src/backend/worker.rs::send_files` applies them to the first file only. Follow-up review explicitly requires staged order, including mixed pictures and file/document runs. Go now flushes files before a picture. This follows CP-016's first-attachment rule; the checked-in Rust `send_pending` itself still buffers file runs past pictures, so this is not a claim of matching that implementation quirk.
- A failed first command keeps all inputs and toasts the error. If a later command fails, the accepted prefix cannot be recalled: only its inputs have been removed, and the failed batch and unattempted suffix remain. Retrying does not duplicate an accepted file, caption or quote. Image and voice blobs transfer ownership only on success.
- Fail-before: `TestFilesSendInABIBatches` sent zero commands for 65 files/documents. All ten text/edit/files/image/voice cases in `TestSynchronousSendFailureKeepsInputs` failed on the original code, using both a real ABI encode error and an immediate core refusal. The voice test also checks a failed retry, blob readability, and successful retry after removing the fault.
- Fault proof: temporarily raising the batch limit to 65 failed `TestFilesSendInABIBatches`, `TestLaterBatchFailureKeepsOnlyUnsentInputs` and the mixed-dispatch test (now `TestFileBatchesPreserveStagedOrder`); restored to 64. No source-text assertions or framework changes.
- Original fix (`fb0f6af`) gates: gofmt and diff checks clean; vet on darwin/linux/windows, with and without `gnui_release`; all 40 test packages pass, including the 356/356 layout sweep; live.sh's 16/16 tests and shell live `-race` pass, as do the focused send tests under `-race`. One direct `-demo -bench -gate -allocsites` run exits 0: 12 measured sessions, 3,662 steady frames, **0 steady allocations**, no gate failures (118 action allocations allowed). Evidence logs: temporary `parity-close/fix-send-*` files, not committed.

### Follow-up review: picture preparation (2026-10-22)

- `paste.init` formerly replaced the completion channel while workers kept the old one. `TestPendingPictureSurvivesWindowRecreation` failed for both open and parked pictures; `TestRemovedPendingPictureIsFreedAfterRecreation` showed the completed blob was stranded. The tests hold a real PNG worker at `PutBlob`, create a replacement offscreen window, then release it. - Preparation formerly discarded the original PNG and error, marked only `Failed`, and Send then removed the picture, caption and quote. `TestFailedPicturePreparationKeepsInputsAndRetries` and `TestInvalidPNGStaysStagedUntilRemoved` failed before the fix. Failed inputs now retain their PNG, error chip and draft/reply; another explicit Send retries preparation off-thread. Failure cancels a queued send rather than retrying every frame. Removing an invalid picture unblocks sending the caption alone.
- Upstream `stage_clipboard` stages already decoded RGBA, not a pending PNG worker, so there is no identical Rust decode-failure state. The preservation requirement here follows the coordinator's explicit instruction; upstream's refused images separately retain their pixels/caption/quote.

### Follow-up review: voice preparation (2026-10-22)

### Session-scoped media regression (2026-10-22, z12-voice)

- Root cause: unlink/chat cleanup discarded recording activity but not retained voice, and account removal had no composer listener. Voice jobs and retained inputs identified their owner by a reused account pointer, so a late preparation could restore or send old PCM through a relinked number. Parked failed-PNG entries also survived cleanup.
- Unlink, `AccountRemoved` and `ChatRemoved` now retire only the matching account/chat's retained PCM, blobs, failed PNGs and finishing recorder. Voice jobs have cancellable tickets; this also rejects a deleted chat's completion after that JID is recreated. Every PNG/voice preparation captures `Account.Serial`; stale completions are rejected before send, restore or toast, and their blobs are freed through the original connection. Retained voice uses the same session check when offered/retried.
- Fail-before: `TestMediaIsDroppedWhenItsOwnerEnds` exercises unlink/removal/chat deletion with held, parked and hidden-account media, both PCM and blobs; `TestStaleMediaPreparationCannotReachANewOwner` holds real preparation workers across those events and relinking, including success and failure completions and independent Serial changes. `TestUnlinkCancelsFinishingVoice` covers the recorder before conversion starts. `TestRetainedVoiceRequiresItsOriginalSession` verifies no retry through a replacement core and freeing through the original core. `TestUnrelatedLifecycleDoesNotCancelVoicePreparation` protects other accounts/chats from overbroad cancellation.
- Fault proof: disabling owner cleanup/session guards and freeing through the slot's current connection makes the lifecycle/session tests fail again. Faults were reverted. No state, core, ABI or framework code changed.
- Validation on base `9f98f8b2`: composer tests pass with and without `gnui_release`; full composer race passes; all six platform/tag vet variants pass; the full suite passes all 40 packages and layout 356/356. Gofmt and diff checks are clean. ## Microphone hand test (CP-023, CP-024, CP-025)

This needs a bundled build whose Info.plist has `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription`: `gnui run` with a `gnui/gnui.json` as described in the plan's packaging section.

1. Reset the answer with `tccutil reset Microphone ai.a3lab.zap.dev`, then start the bundled app, linked to a test account.
2. Open a chat and click the microphone in the empty field, where Send goes. macOS asks for the microphone. Choose **Allow**. Recording starts with no second click: the bar shows Discard, a pulsing red light, `0:00` counting up, the waveform and Send.
3. Speak for about 3 seconds. The waveform's bars rise with your voice and stay low while you're silent. Click **Send**. The bar goes away, the field comes back, and the phone receives a voice note about 3 s long.
4. Choose Reply on a message, click the microphone, speak, and press **Enter**. The voice note arrives quoting that message.
5. Click the microphone, speak, and press **Escape**. Nothing is sent. Repeat, but lock the app (Lock now) while recording: nothing is sent, and the recording is gone after unlocking.
6. Click the microphone and click Send within half a second. Nothing is sent and no toast appears.
7. Whisper a voice note and send it. It plays at normal loudness on the phone.
8. In System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, turn ZapFast off. Click the microphone. A toast reads "Could not record: microphone access is off for ZapFast (System Settings, Privacy & Security, Microphone)", and System Settings opens on the Microphone page.
9. Run unbundled (`go run ./cmd/zapfast -core …`) and click the microphone. A toast reads "Could not record: this build cannot ask for the microphone (run ZapFast from its app bundle)".
10. With Wi-Fi off, record and send. When the refusal arrives, "Voice message (0:0N) not sent" waits above the field and Send replaces the microphone. Turn Wi-Fi on and click Send: the voice note is sent. Do it again and press Enter instead: it is sent too.
